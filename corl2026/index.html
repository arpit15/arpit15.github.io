Collection status Unofficial

CoRL 2026 announced its decisions but has not published the accepted-paper list, and OpenReview keeps submissions private. This list combines arXiv papers whose comments or journal references mention CoRL 2026 with papers announced on X and LinkedIn (#corl2026, “accepted to CoRL”). Duplicates are merged by arXiv ID and then by title. Each paper's status comes from its arXiv comment or, when stronger, from the authors' post.
Accepted: the comment says so explicitly ("Accepted to CoRL 2026", "To appear", "Spotlight"). Likely: the comment only tags "CoRL 2026", which usually signals acceptance but is sometimes added at submission time. Submitted, Workshop and Unverified papers are listed for completeness and are hidden from the paper list by default.
